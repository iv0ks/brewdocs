# Steaming silky microfoam

Microfoam is milk with thousands of tiny, uniform bubbles — glossy and
paint-like, not stiff and bubbly. Two phases get you there: stretching and
texturing.

## Stretch, then texture

- Stretch: with the tip just below the surface, introduce air for a second or
  two. You want a gentle "tss tss", not a loud screech.
- Texture: sink the wand slightly deeper and angle it to spin the milk into a
  smooth whirlpool. No more air — just polish.

## Temperature

Stop at around 60–65 C (the jug is too hot to hold comfortably for more than a
second or two). Overheated milk tastes flat and loses its sweetness.

## Finish

Tap the jug to pop large bubbles, swirl to reincorporate, and pour while the
milk is still shiny. If it has started to separate, swirl again before pouring.
