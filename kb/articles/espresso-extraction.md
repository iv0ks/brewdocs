# Dialling in the perfect espresso shot

A great shot is a balance of three variables: grind size, dose (how much coffee
goes in) and yield (how much espresso comes out). Change one and the others
follow.

## Start from a recipe

- Dose: 18 g in a double basket
- Yield: 36 g out (a 1:2 ratio)
- Time: 25 to 30 seconds from the moment you hit the button

## Reading the shot

If it runs too fast and tastes sharp or sour, the grind is too coarse — go
finer. If it drips, stalls and tastes bitter or ashy, the grind is too fine —
go coarser. Adjust in small steps and change only one thing at a time.

## Consistency beats perfection

Weigh your dose and your yield every time. A cheap scale is the single biggest
upgrade most people can make to their espresso. Once the numbers are stable,
your palate has something to calibrate against.
