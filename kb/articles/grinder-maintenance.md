# Weekly grinder maintenance

Your grinder has more impact on the cup than your machine. A little upkeep keeps
it tasting clean and grinding consistently.

## Weekly

- Brush the burrs and chute to clear built-up fines and oils.
- Run a small amount of grinder-cleaning tablets if your model allows it.
- Wipe the hopper; stale oils there taint fresh beans.

## Retention

Most grinders hold a few grams of old grounds between the burrs. Purge a gram or
two before the first shot of the day so you are not brewing yesterday's coffee.

## Recalibrate after a bean change

New beans — especially a different roast level — almost always need a grind
adjustment. Expect to re-dial whenever you open a fresh bag.
