# How the Brewdocs loyalty program works

Earn a stamp with every drink and turn them into free coffee. Here is the short
version.

## Earning stamps

- One stamp per handcrafted drink, in store or ordered ahead in the app.
- Stamps land in your account the moment your order is marked ready.

## Rewards

- 9 stamps = one free drink of your choice, any size.
- Rewards never expire while your account is active.

## Linking a card to the app

If you collected stamps on a physical card, open the app, go to Rewards and tap
"Link a card". Enter the code on the back of the card once and your stamps move
over automatically.
